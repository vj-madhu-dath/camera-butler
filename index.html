MEET YOUR CAMERA BUTLER

Your eye. A little assistance.

  1. Choose a shooting style for practical angle advice.
  2. Enable your camera, then tap your subject. The green target shows a suggested composition point.
  3. Reframe and tap again. Selected points are not automatically tracked.
  4. Enable AI guidance for live object detection, or try the sample. AI boxes update automatically; tap the viewfinder to switch back to manual selection.
  5. Enable the horizon sensor on a supported phone. Capture saves a clean image without guides.

Camera access requires localhost or HTTPS. For phone use, serve this app over trusted HTTPS. Camera and motion availability depend on your device and browser. AI models are served locally with the app; no images leave your browser.
